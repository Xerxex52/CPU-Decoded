Questions

Do I need to know electronics already?+

No. Start with bits and logic gates. Missions gradually introduce calculations, registers, memory and instructions. Lessons help you revisit a concept.

Do I really build a processor?+

You assemble and explore the building blocks of an educational 4-bit processor, then program the CPU in the game. ROM and RAM are shown separately. This is a simulation to understand the principles, not an electronics hardware kit.

How do missions and the workshop differ?+

Missions provide a goal and tests. The workshop lets you choose modules, create circuits and save projects.

Do I need an internet connection or account?+

After downloading, the game works offline without an account. No extra hardware is needed. Progress and projects are stored locally, without syncing between devices.

What does the €7.99 include?+

All missions, the workshop, lessons and programming. €7.99 is the planned price in France; prices elsewhere will be shown on the App Store.
